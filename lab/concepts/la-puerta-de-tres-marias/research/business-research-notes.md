## 4. Offerings

**Cuisine (established, multi-source):** traditional/Mexican home cooking. Restaurant Guru: "Mexican cuisine"; pos.do category "Mexicano"; 2019 press article describes "Platillos hechos en cazuelas de barro" (dishes made in clay pots) and breakfasts from $80 (https://www.24morelos.com/ya-visitaste-la-puerta-de-tres-marias/, published 2019-06-14, third-party).

**Meal windows:** reviewers repeatedly describe it as a breakfast/lunch/dinner spot ("Un lugar para desayunar, comer y cenar", pos.do review by Leondarck, 5 stars, observed 2026-09-20). Breakfast prominence is corroborated by the 2019 article's breakfast pricing. Inference, not a business statement.

**Menu availability:**
- Physical menu on site: implied by reviews, not verified.
- Online menu: **Uber Eats Cuernavaca store page exists** and is indexed with the title "La Puerta de Tres Marias | Menu | Cuernavaca | App de Uber Eats" (https://www.ubereats.com/mx/store/la-puerta-de-tres-marias/LKzmDljiSZqVPQ8tfwsj8g). The page returned **403** to fetch, so **no item names or prices were read from it**. Do not quote prices attributed to Uber Eats.
- Aggregator menu pages were empty or blocked: Restaurant Guru's menu sub-page https://es.restaurantguru.com/La-Puerta-De-tres-Marias-Cuernavaca/menu reports **no items and prices, only 8 user-uploaded menu photos**; https://carta.menu/restaurants/cuernavaca/la-puerta-de-tres-marias returned 403; pos.do's menu sub-page returned **410 Gone**.
- Posted photos of menus: yes, 8 user-uploaded menu images per Restaurant Guru (2026-09-20). Provenance is diners, not the business.

**Dishes.** Business-controlled specificity is thin. The clearest is a group-page Facebook video of enchiladas de mole whose caption fragment reads "El mole de la Puerta, tiene el sazon unico que la abuela nos ha..." (https://www.facebook.com/LaPuertaDeTresMarias/videos/enchiladas-de-mole/203848901035989/, indexed 2020-04-03, observed 2026-09-20). This is a **group-level** claim about house mole and is not specific to the Cuernavaca kitchen, so use it only with that caveat.

Review-sourced **individual impressions** (do not present as signature dishes): "Las enchiladas de moles muy sabrosas" (4 stars, Lesli, Restaurant Guru, 2026-09-20); "Excelente sazon unos taquitos de la casa de sabor formidable" (4 stars, Marco Lazaro, pos.do, 2026-09-20); "El cafe es muy rico, prueben el de olla... Los chilaquiles..." (5 stars, Ozz VG, pos.do, 2026-09-20); "Everything delicious!!! I ate bone marrow soup" (5 stars, Paty, Restaurant Guru, 2026-09-20).

pos.do renders a long dish list for this branch (mole, enchiladas, chilaquiles, cafe de olla, pozole, tamales, quesadillas, huaraches, sopes, tampiqueña, barbacoa, conejo, pancita, lengua a la veracruzana, consomé de borrego, sopa de médula, huevos "la puerta", verdolagas en verde, chicharron en salsa verde, and others). **Provenance caution:** this looks like an algorithmically assembled dish pool, it includes items that read like the mountain-branch repertoire (trout/lamb/barbacoa country) and even odd entries, and it is not price-verified. Treat the whole list as **unverified aggregation**. Only `cafe de olla`, `chilaquiles`, `taquitos de la casa`, `mole/enchiladas de mole` and `sopa/consome de borrego` recur across independent reviews, so those are the defensible ones.

**No dish should appear in site copy as a signature claim unless it is priced or listed by the business itself.** None of the above is currently price-verified.

4. **TripAdvisor review volume for this branch:** 6 (Restaurant Guru) vs 3 (pos.do).
5. **Price level:** qualitative `€€` / `$$` only. The single review-level figures found (`$38.00 huevo adicional`, `25 pesos` parking, both pos.do, 2026-09-20) are **diner recollections**, and the 2019 "desayunos desde $80" is stale by seven years. No current price was verified anywhere.

7. **Review dates:** pos.do stamps every review it shows as `2026-05-20`, which is almost certainly its crawl date rather than the review dates. Do not use review dates.
